Frequently Asked Questions

Standard check-in is at 3:00 PM and check-out is at 11:00 AM. Early check-in and late check-out may be available upon request and are complimentary for direct booking guests (subject to availability).

Yes! As a direct booking guest, you get priority access to early check-in and late check-out at no additional charge (subject to availability). Simply message us at the time of booking and we'll do our best to accommodate you.

Flexible policy: Free cancellation up to 48 hours before check-in for a full refund. Cancellations within 48 hours of check-in forfeit the first night's rate. No-shows forfeit the full booking amount.

The Grand Retreat includes free private parking. The Luxe Suite has available street parking. The Urban Oasis has access to nearby paid parking garages. Full parking details are included in your welcome guide upon booking.

We welcome well-behaved pets at The Grand Retreat (additional $50 pet fee applies). The Luxe Suite and The Urban Oasis are pet-free properties. Please contact us before booking to discuss your needs.

No events or parties are permitted at any MAEZ Collection property. Our spaces are designed for quiet, comfortable stays for registered guests only. Violation of this policy results in immediate termination of the stay with no refund.

All properties feature keyless self check-in via smart lock. After completing your booking, you'll receive a unique entry code 24 hours before check-in. No key exchange needed — arrive at your convenience after the check-in time.

Absolutely no hidden fees. We show your complete price breakdown before checkout: nightly rate + cleaning fee + taxes. The total you see is the total you pay. Booking direct saves you Airbnb's 14–16% service fees.
